How to install

DownloadTap “Download for Android” on your phone. Chrome may ask to keep the file; tap Download.
Open the fileTap the downloaded MyCard.io file in your notifications or Downloads.
Allow installIf asked, tap Settings and turn on “Allow from this source”, then go back.
Install & openTap Install. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details → Install anyway.

What it does

Smart receipt scannerAI reads merchant, items, discount, VAT, service charge, fees, tip and total.
Business cards, translatedCards in Japanese, Bangla, Chinese and more are saved in English with the original kept.
Loyalty walletKeep membership cards with a scannable barcode or QR code, right on your phone.
Privacy: photos are only used to read the details and are never saved. Only the text data is kept. iPhone version coming later.
